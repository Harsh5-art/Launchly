Refund Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Because the Playbook is a digital file you can download straight away, all sales are final once the PDF has been delivered, except in the cases below.

When you can get a refund

  • Charged twice: If you were charged more than once for the same order, we refund the extra payment in full.
  • Can't get your PDF: If the payment went through but you couldn't download the PDF, and we can't fix it within 7 days of you contacting us, we refund you in full.
  • Payment failed but money was deducted: If the money left your account but no order was confirmed, it is usually returned automatically by your bank or Razorpay within 5–7 working days. If not, contact us.

When refunds are not given

  • You changed your mind after downloading the PDF.
  • You expected different content than what is described on the website.
  • You did not get the results you hoped for.

How to ask for a refund

Email harshkalantri202@gmail.com within 7 days of your purchase with your Razorpay payment ID (it's on your Razorpay receipt and on the download page) and a short note on the problem.

How long it takes

Approved refunds are sent back to your original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 working days.
