Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what information Launchly collects when you visit this website or buy The Digital Product Playbook, and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Payment details: Payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay shares your name, email, phone number and payment ID with us so we can confirm your order.
  • Your browser: After a successful payment, this website saves your payment ID in your browser's local storage. This lets the "My Download" page recognise you on the same browser. It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time.
  • Messages you send us: If you email us, we keep your email address and message so we can reply.

How we use it

  • To confirm your payment and deliver your PDF.
  • To answer support requests and process refunds.
  • To meet tax and legal record-keeping rules.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Third-party services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Razorpay for payments and Google Fonts for text styles. These services may log technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as Indian tax law requires. Support emails are deleted when no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing harshkalantri202@gmail.com.
